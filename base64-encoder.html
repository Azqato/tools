Base64 Encoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode it back. Accents, emoji and CJK all survive the round trip. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Base64

The URL-safe variant swaps + and / for - and _ and drops the = padding, which is what most URL and filename consumers expect. Decoding accepts either alphabet whichever box it came from.
