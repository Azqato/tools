Wash Sale Tracker

Add the ticker and the date you traded, and the tool counts the 30 day window forward for you. Everything stays in this browser.

Pick the date from the calendar or type it in. The window is counted as the trade date plus 30 calendar days, weekends and holidays included.

Active Wash Sales

Expired Wash Sales

This is a record keeping aid, not tax advice. It tracks dates you enter and counts forward from them. It does not know what you traded, whether a sale was at a loss, or whether a wash sale actually occurred, and it does not account for the 30 days before a sale that the rule also covers. Check anything that matters with your broker's records or an accountant.
